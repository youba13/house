Our Story

Homemade Dumplings,
Grandma's Recipes

At Pelmeni Haus, every dumpling is handcrafted with the same love and care that Russian grandmothers have passed down for generations. We believe that the best food comes from the heart — and it shows in every bite.

Our kitchen brings together the rich culinary traditions of Russia, Ukraine, and Eastern Europe. From classic Siberian pelmeni stuffed with juicy pork and beef, to delicate vareniki filled with cottage cheese or cherries, to crispy chebureks — every dish tells a story of home.

"We tasted chebureks which were very large and delicious — a true taste of authentic Eastern European comfort food."

Whether you are craving a warming bowl of Soljanka soup, a hearty Chicken Pot Pie, or a plate of perfectly boiled dumplings topped with sour cream and fresh dill, Pelmeni Haus delivers flavors that transport you straight to a cozy kitchen in Moscow or Kyiv.
